This website is about Management Information Systems (MIS) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) Ethics. MIS is a information system created to aid in decision making, analyses, and visualization within a company. The goal of a Management Information System is to increase the profitability and productivity of an organization by providing the management the support it needs to be efficient. AI is a computer system that preforms tasks that typically would require human intelligence. AI systems can process vast amounts of data, identify patterns humans might miss, and make decisions based on the information collected. MIS and AI work together to aid companies in making quicker, and efficient decisions, and to keep them competitive in the market.
