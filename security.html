Malware and Cyber Threats

An example of malware and cyber threats is the WannaCry Ransomware Attack (May 12, 2017) was a worldwide cyberattack by the WannaCry ransomware cryptoworm, who targeted computers running the Microsoft Windows operating system by encrypting data and demanding ransom payments in the Bitcoin cryptocurrency. The impact it had affected more than 200,000 computers across numerous countries. It hurt hospitals, government agencies, and businesses. This event conbined a self-propagating computer worm with stolen intelligence agency exploits to cripple global infranstructure in a matter of hours.
