Correct AI Usage

Using AI the right way is important to ensure that it is used responsibly and following ethical rules. AI should be used to enhance human ideas, not to replace them. An example of incorrect usage is to use AI to create fake images and call them real. Using AI honestly and within the rules is important for the correct use.

Incorrect AI Usage

Plagerism
Using AI to copy someone else's work without permission

Academic Dishonesty
Using AI on assignments when prohibited

Misinformation
Using or sharing false AI-generated information without verifying its accuracy

I believe that AI is a useful tool when used ethically. However, using AI for plagerism, academic dishonesty, and misinformation is not ethical. The correct use of AI is to enhance human ideas, not to replace them. I think AI is a great way to improve productivity and efficiency.
